<p>SeaSeaS, a CO<span class="sub2">2</span> logistics and infrastructure development company, has awarded McDermott the pre-front-end-engineering and design (Pre-FEED) contract of a first-of-a-kind open-access liquid CO<span class="sub2">2</span> terminal to be built in the Port of Amsterdam.</p>
<p>The terminal is being developed as a multimodal CO<span class="sub2">2</span> accumulation and export hub, designed to receive liquefied CO<span class="sub2">2</span> from industrial emitters in the Netherlands and Germany via rail, river barges, and trucks with onward transport to offshore North Sea storage sites via seagoing vessels.</p>
<p>By combining SeaSeaS’ CO<span class="sub2">2</span> logistics and infrastructure development focus with McDermott’s global front-end engineering and project delivery capabilities, the collaboration aims to accelerate concept definition, technical maturation and investment readiness of the project towards taking FID by 2027.</p>
<h2>Access to offshore CO<span class="sub2">2</span> storage in the North Sea</h2>
<p>Europe’s industrial decarbonisation trajectory requires a rapid scale-up of carbon capture, transport and storage infrastructure. EU policy frameworks under the Net-Zero Industry Act set a target of at least 50 million tonnes per year of CO<span class="sub2">2</span> injection capacity by 2030, with significantly higher volumes required by 2040 and 2050.</p>
<p>SeaSeaS Amsterdam is designed to support emitters that are not directly connected to CO<span class="sub2">2</span> pipeline systems. The open-access terminal concept allows industrial companies to aggregate volumes and access multiple offshore storage sinks via ship transport, while also enabling potential future integration with regional CO<span class="sub2">2</span> pipeline networks and connection to supply  future Carbon Capturing and Utilization (CCU) initiatives in the port .</p>
<p>The project was awarded a TSE (Topsector Energy) Industry Studies grant from the Dutch Government in December 2025 to support its development toward investment decision readiness.</p>
<h2>Pre-FEED</h2>
<p>The pre-FEED represents the pre-engineering stage prior to a strategic investment decision (SID). Under the contract, McDermott will perform basic engineering and design services to define and optimise the technical specification of the plant as well as cost estimating services.  SeaSeaS will continue to lead project development, stakeholder engagement, commercial structuring and permitting strategy.</p>
<p>McDermott will support technical development activities including:</p>
<ul><li>Concept definition and design basis development</li><li>Pre-FEED and FEED planning</li><li>Cost estimating and execution strategy</li><li>Technical input for permitting documentation</li><li>Preparation for procurement and construction phases</li></ul>
<p>The parties intend to structure the development phase to support a bankable project framework aligned with industrial customer requirements and future CO<span class="sub2">2</span> transport and storage integration.</p>
<figure class="quote"><figcaption>Kees van Seventer, Co-founder and Managing Director SeaSeaS:</figcaption><blockquote>“Decarbonising the industry requires more than capturing technology. It requires logistics, infrastructure and strong partnership solutions across this complex value chain. By working with McDermott, we are strengthening the engineering foundation behind SeaSeaS Amsterdam and accelerating the pathway toward a robust, open-access CO<span class="sub2">2</span> export solution for industrial emitters in North-West Europe.”</blockquote></figure>
<h2>Strengthening the Dutch CO<span class="sub2">2</span> ecosystem</h2>
<p>The announcement comes amid broader momentum in Dutch and European carbon management infrastructure. National climate targets and industrial policy frameworks increasingly recognise the importance of CO<span class="sub2">2</span> transport and storage systems in achieving emissions reductions while maintaining industrial competitiveness.</p>
<p>SeaSeaS Amsterdam is positioned to complement existing and planned CO<span class="sub2">2</span> supply chains by offering multimodal flexibility and aggregation capabilities, particularly for inland and mid-sized industrial emitters.</p>
<p>SeaSeaS is working closely with The Port of Amsterdam and NZKG, parties that are actively developing CO<span class="sub2">2</span> network initiatives, reinforcing the region’s role in Europe’s emerging carbon management landscape.</p>
